Question 1 (Domain 1: Security & Risk Management)

A multi-national healthcare provider has identified a critical business process with an unacceptably high residual risk. Senior leadership wants the FIRST step that aligns with CISSP risk governance. What is the MOST appropriate action?

A. Direct the technical team to implement compensating controls immediately
B. Present risk treatment options and residual risk implications to the risk owner for a decision
C. Purchase cyber insurance to transfer the risk without further analysis
D. Document the risk and accept it based on operational urgency

Correct Answer: B

Explanation:
The BEST answer is to present treatment options and residual risk implications to the risk owner so the accountable decision-maker can choose the treatment consistent with governance. Implementing controls or transferring risk without a risk-owner decision bypasses governance, and acceptance requires explicit authorization. CISSP Exam Tip: Risk acceptance is a business decision, not a technical one.

Question 2 (Domain 1: Security & Risk Management)

A financial services firm is updating its information security documentation. Which document should define the REQUIRED baseline configuration for endpoint hardening across the enterprise?

A. Policy
B. Standard
C. Procedure
D. Guideline

Correct Answer: B

Explanation:
A standard sets mandatory, measurable requirements like baseline configurations. Policies provide high-level direction, procedures outline step-by-step tasks, and guidelines are optional. CISSP Exam Tip: Policies state intent; standards specify mandatory requirements.

Question 3 (Domain 1: Security & Risk Management)

After a breach, the board asks for a long-term response that aligns security with business objectives. Which activity is the PRIMARY governance responsibility?

A. Defining enterprise security strategy and risk appetite
B. Selecting specific security tools for implementation
C. Coordinating incident response playbooks
D. Reviewing system configuration baselines

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Governance sets strategy and risk appetite to align security with business objectives. Tool selection and operational activities are management tasks. CISSP Exam Tip: Governance provides direction; management executes.

Question 4 (Domain 1: Security & Risk Management)

A cloud service provider conducts a business impact analysis and discovers a critical service with a four-hour RTO. Which is the MOST appropriate next step?

A. Implement a disaster recovery plan with recovery strategies aligned to the RTO
B. Update the incident response plan to include media communications
C. Perform penetration testing of the critical service
D. Transfer the service to a third party without contract review

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
The BIA drives recovery strategy selection; implementing DR capabilities aligned to the RTO is the logical next step. Incident response and testing are related but do not directly ensure recovery requirements. CISSP Exam Tip: BIA outputs drive recovery objectives and strategy selection.

Question 5 (Domain 1: Security & Risk Management)

A manufacturing company processes personal data across multiple jurisdictions. The CISO is asked for the PRIMARY purpose of a compliance program. What is the BEST answer?

A. Ensure technical controls meet vendor benchmarks
B. Demonstrate adherence to applicable laws and regulations
C. Optimize system performance and availability
D. Replace risk management with checklist assessments

Correct Answer: B

Explanation:
Compliance programs ensure the organization can demonstrate adherence to relevant laws and regulations. Benchmarks and performance are secondary considerations, and compliance does not replace risk management. CISSP Exam Tip: Compliance is necessary but not sufficient for security.

Question 6 (Domain 1: Security & Risk Management)

A security manager discovers evidence that a senior executive bypassed security controls. What is the MOST appropriate CISSP-aligned response?

A. Ignore the incident to avoid reputational impact
B. Report the issue through established governance and ethics channels
C. Confront the executive directly and demand remediation
D. Publicly disclose the issue to pressure compliance

Correct Answer: B

Explanation:
CISSP ethics emphasize integrity and adherence to established reporting channels. Direct confrontation or public disclosure bypasses governance, and ignoring violates professional duty. CISSP Exam Tip: Follow formal reporting paths to maintain integrity and accountability.
